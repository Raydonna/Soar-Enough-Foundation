Our Story

From a Small Vision to a Growing Impact

Soar Enough Foundation is an organization like no other. We are a faith-driven nonprofit committed to transforming lives through education, health, empowerment, and sports. We believe sports are more than games, they are powerful tools to build discipline, teamwork, resilience, and leadership. From nurturing young athletes to promoting wellness for all ages, we create opportunities for people to grow in both body and spirit. Our mission is to empower the next generation to soar in faith and purpose, while improving health and well-being across communities. At Soar Enough, we believe Jesus is Enough to Soar on and off the field.

Guiding Principles

Vision & Mission

Our Vision

To build a world where youths and adults are empowered through education, sport, health, and faith in Jesus, creating communities of purpose, wellness and impact.

Our Mission

To inspire and equip young athletes, adults and communities by promoting education, mentoring, sport development and faith-based values, and holistic health, helping every individual soar beyond limitations and live purposeful, healthy lives.
